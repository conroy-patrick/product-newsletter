01The Portal
The Front Door

Arc Portal: the central operational hub for Arc

The Beghou Arc Portal is a single, unified entry point for clients, partners, and internal teams. One login, one modern interface — covering every Arc module and the legacy tools alongside them, including Commercial Targeting, Goal Setting, Roster, Tableau, CRM, Databricks, AQM, Affiliations, BI tools, and the User Guides. Nuvalent is the first production client (live now), with Scholar Rock in active migration and StealthBio, Servier, and Celcuity queued behind them. Available today, the Portal has moved from a demo-stage product to a single operational hub. Release 2 makes it the primary front door for Arc.

02Available Now
Available Today

What’s available today: twelve capability areas

Arc Portal delivers twelve capability areas today, ready for any client to turn on. Single sign-on through Beghou’s enterprise identity provider. Multi-tenant isolation, keeping each client’s users, branding, and dashboard separate. Three platform roles plus per-app product roles, enforced server-side on every endpoint. And the piece that makes the rollout possible at all — a Downstream Sync Service that pushes every access change to legacy provisioning systems with reliable delivery, automatic retries, and a full audit trail. Nuvalent runs the production deployment now, with teams adopting each capability as their workflows call for it.

Capabilities available today
  • Sign-on & identity. SSO through Beghou’s enterprise IdP, rate-limited login, one-time-code step-up for sensitive actions, one-click into every assigned app.
  • Multi-tenancy. Each client fully isolated — its own users, apps, branding, and dashboard. Beghou admins see across; client admins see only their own tenant.
  • User management. Single-add, bulk CSV upload with column mapping, soft-delete only, profile-picture upload with in-browser cropping.
  • Roles & permissions. Global Admin / ClientAdmin / RegularUser, plus per-app product roles. Server-enforced on every endpoint — the UI cannot be bypassed.
  • App & deployment catalogue. Beghou-delivered apps registered once with role taxonomies; per-client deployments carry their own URL, version, support contact, and icon.
  • Access assignment. Grant, revoke, and bulk-manage per-user / per-app role bindings.
  • Per-client branding. Logo upload (desktop + mobile) and a primary-colour picker that re-themes the UI in real time.
  • Dashboard. Per-client, tile-based, responsive (1/2/3 columns), drag-positioned, with custom-HTML tiles gated on role and deployment access.
  • Templates Library + Code Playground. Per-client HTML widget management with an in-browser editor and live preview iframe.
  • Downstream Sync Service. Every access change pushed to legacy provisioning systems with reliable delivery, automatic retries, per-event status tracking, and a full per-attempt audit trail.
  • Sync Audit Log UI. Filterable grid of every sync attempt, with operation badges, expandable detail rows, and full request / response visibility.
  • Observability + security hardening. Server-side auth on every endpoint, sanitised uploads, security headers, CORS allow-list, health-check endpoints, structured logging with correlation IDs.

03What’s Next
In Build — Release 2

From sign-in to running: Calendar + Arc DE permissioning

Release 2 — in BRD now, owned by Siva Kristipati — adds two capabilities that move the Portal from “the place clients sign in” to “the place clients run from.” Together they consolidate internal product access under one Portal-driven model and remove the need for anyone — internal or client — to drop into back-end systems to grant, revoke, or schedule.

Up next on the Portal
  • Calendar Module. Native client-facing event scheduling on the Portal. Client admins upload events; all client users see them in real time, with tenant isolation, audit logging, and admin-only write access. Requested by the Nuvalent team; committed for end of Q3; #1 priority and the immediate next feature in build.
  • Arc DE permissioning from the Portal. Databricks-based Arc DE RBAC managed entirely through Portal admin controls, propagated via a Function App intermediary, with auto-revoke on user deactivation and separation-of-duties enforced (users cannot modify their own permissions). Consolidates internal product access under one Portal-driven model.

Use Case — Nuvalent

The first production proof: Nuvalent on Arc Portal

The Nuvalent go-live is the first end-to-end proof of the model. Beghou admins provisioned, branded, and stood up the client tenant without engineering rebuilding anything for that one deployment. Every access change flowed through the Downstream Sync Service into the legacy apps.
